## Demo shot list (30–45 seconds)

Record in this order, no cuts inside a step. Total under one minute.

1. Land on `/tools/idea-validator`. Read the intro line: "Validate before you build." (~2 seconds)
2. Open the "Load an example" dropdown, pick **AI Launch Checklist Builder**. Fields populate. (~4 seconds)
3. Scroll down through the form. Do not scrub — pace it so the viewer sees the eight sections. (~10 seconds)
4. Click Calculate. (~1 second)
5. Result panel appears. Camera stays on the total and verdict for two beats: **79 / 100**, **Validate with landing page/interviews**. (~4 seconds)
6. Scroll to Category breakdown. Let the eye catch two or three bars. (~6 seconds)
7. Scroll to Strongest and Weakest areas. Pause on "Weakest areas to validate". (~6 seconds)
8. Highlight one recommended next action. Read it aloud in the caption, not on screen. (~4 seconds)
9. Click Copy summary. Show the "Copied!" state. Done. (~2 seconds)

Do not edit the demo to inflate the score. The sample above scores exactly 79/100 — that is the honest, verifiable run.
