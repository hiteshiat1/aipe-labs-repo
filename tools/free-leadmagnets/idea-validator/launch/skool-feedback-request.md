## Post Body

We shipped the AI Idea Validator. It is the first free public AIPE Labs tool — an interactive version of the idea validation canvas.

It is not an AI oracle. It is a checklist with a scoring model. The point is to force you to answer eight questions honestly and get a structured verdict before you burn a weekend building.

Before we invest in v1, we need feedback from builders who actually use it.

---

### What I would like you to do

1. Open the tool.
2. Enter a real idea you are considering — not a joke, not a well-worn one.
3. Score yourself honestly on all eight dimensions. Do not inflate.
4. Read the verdict and the category breakdown.
5. Read the strongest and weakest areas.
6. Read the three recommended next actions.
7. Click Copy summary and paste the summary in the comments below.

Optional: also load one of the built-in sample ideas from the "Load an example" dropdown to see how a fully filled-in result looks before you run your own.

Should take under 10 minutes.

---

### What I would like you to tell me

Answer as many as you can. Rough replies beat polished silence — one sentence per question is enough.

1. Which question in the form was hardest for you to answer honestly?
2. Which scoring category felt least useful for your idea?
3. Did the verdict feel right? If not, what would you have expected?
4. Did the "Weakest areas to validate" list help you see what needs testing next?
5. Were the three recommended next actions specific enough to act on this week?
6. What did you expect the tool to do that it did not?
7. Would you use this before starting a new project? Why or why not?
