# Free AI Idea Validation Canvas

Related issue: #6  
Asset type: Free lead magnet  
Version: 1.0

---

## Who This Is For

Use this canvas if you have an AI product, SaaS, app, workflow automation or digital product idea and want to decide whether it is worth building.

This is especially useful if you can already create a quick demo with AI tools, but you are unsure whether the idea deserves serious product engineering effort.

---

## How To Use This Canvas

Fill each section honestly. Do not try to prove your idea is good. Try to discover whether the problem is real, painful and worth solving.

At the end, score the idea out of 100 and choose one verdict:

- Build MVP
- Validate first
- Research more
- Pause or reshape

# 6. Customer Interview Questions

Ask these before building too much.

1. How do you currently handle this problem?
2. How often does this problem happen?
3. What is frustrating about your current solution?
4. What happens if you do nothing?
5. Have you paid for tools or help to solve this before?
6. What would make you switch to a new solution?
7. What would make you not trust this product?
8. Who else is involved in the decision?
9. What would a perfect solution look like?
10. Can I show you a rough version when it is ready?
