## Post Body

Most AI builders do not fail because they cannot create a prototype.

They fail because they build too quickly without checking whether the problem is real, painful and worth solving.

Before you spend days or weeks building another AI app, use this free **AI Idea Validation Canvas**.

It helps you answer:

- Who is this for?
- What problem are you solving?
- How painful is the problem?
- What alternatives already exist?
- What assumptions must be true?
- What should the first MVP include?
- Should you build, validate, research or pause?

---

## What The Canvas Includes

- Problem definition
- ICP canvas
- Assumption map
- Competitor snapshot
- Customer interview questions
- MVP boundary
- 100-point idea scoring system
- Build / validate / research / pause verdict

---

## Why This Matters

A cool AI demo is not the same as a validated product.

If your app only works on `localhost:3000`, you have a prototype.

If nobody has confirmed the problem is painful, you have a guess.

This canvas helps you test the guess before you overbuild.
