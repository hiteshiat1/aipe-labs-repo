## 1. Idea Summary

## Idea Name

AI Launch Checklist Builder

## One-Line Description

A simple tool that helps AI-assisted builders turn a prototype into a production launch checklist based on their app type, stack and current blockers.

## Product Type

SaaS / AI workflow tool / template product

---

## 2. Problem Definition

## What problem are you solving?

AI-assisted builders can create prototypes quickly, but they do not know what steps are required to make the product production-ready and launchable.

They often miss architecture, authentication, database design, secrets, CI/CD, monitoring, error handling, security basics and launch planning.

## Who has this problem?

Indie hackers, junior engineers, non-technical founders and AI builders using tools like Claude Code, Cursor, Lovable, Bolt, Replit, Vercel, Firebase and Supabase.

## When does the problem happen?

After the first prototype works locally or as a simple demo, but before the builder is ready to put the product in front of real users.

## What happens if the problem is not solved?

The builder either abandons the idea, ships something fragile, wastes time rebuilding, or launches with serious gaps in reliability, security and usability.

## Problem Severity

Score: 4/5  
Reason: This is not always business-critical, but it is painful enough to block launch and monetisation.

---

## 3. ICP Canvas

## Primary User

AI-assisted builders who have already created a prototype.

## Buyer

The builder themselves. Later, small teams, bootcamps, agencies or communities may pay for a more complete version.

## Segment

Solo AI builders and indie hackers trying to launch their first production-ready software product.

## Current Workflow

They search YouTube, ask ChatGPT, copy scattered checklists, follow random tutorials and guess what needs to be done.

## Trigger Event

They finish a prototype and realise they do not know how to deploy, secure, test, monitor or launch it properly.

## Budget / Willingness To Pay

They may pay £9–£59 for a useful template or checklist. Some may pay more for a review, cohort or implementation support.

---

## 4. Existing Alternatives

| Alternative | What it does well | What is missing | Price / effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Generic ChatGPT checklist | Fast and personalised | Not structured, not reusable, may miss production details | Free but inconsistent |
| YouTube tutorials | Practical examples | Fragmented, stack-specific, hard to adapt | Free but time-consuming |
| Launch checklists | Good for marketing launch | Often weak on engineering readiness | Free/paid |
| DevOps/SRE guides | Strong on ops | Too advanced for builders and founders | High learning effort |

## Why would someone switch to your product?

Because it gives a simple, structured checklist based on their product stage and stack, not generic advice.

## 6. Customer Interview Questions

1. What did you build most recently with AI tools?
2. Did it reach real users or stop at demo stage?
3. What blocked you after the first working version?
4. What production tasks confused you most?
5. Did you use any launch or readiness checklist?
6. What would you expect from a prototype-to-production checklist?
7. Would you prefer a free checklist, Notion template, or review service?
8. Would you pay for a complete launch readiness OS?
9. What stack are you using?
10. Can I review your current prototype stage?

---

## 7. MVP Boundary

## Smallest Useful Version

A simple form/checklist where a builder selects product type, stack and stage, then gets a launch readiness checklist.

## Must Have

- Product type selector
- Stack/stage questions
- Readiness checklist output
- Copyable tasks
- Link to AIPE Labs Skool for support

## Should Have Later

- GitHub issue export
- Notion export
- Score dashboard
- Team collaboration
- Stack-specific deployment guidance

## Do Not Build Yet

- Full SaaS account system
- Payment integration
- Complex AI agent workflow
- Multi-user workspace
- Deep integrations
