## Purpose

The AI Idea Validation Canvas is the first practical lead magnet for AI Product Engineering Labs.

It helps AI-assisted builders, indie hackers, founders and junior engineers decide whether an idea should be built, paused or researched further before spending time creating another prototype.

The asset supports the AIPE Labs audience wedge:

> AIPE Labs is for AI-assisted builders who can create demos but struggle to productionise, deploy, secure, launch and monetise them.

Most builders do not need another prompt. They need a simple validation system that connects idea, user, pain, alternatives, assumptions, MVP scope and launch risk.

## Free Version

The free version helps a builder answer:

> Should I build this idea, pause it, or research more?

It includes:

- Problem definition
- ICP canvas
- Assumption map
- Competitor snapshot
- Customer interview guide
- MVP boundary
- Simple 100-point scoring model
- Build / validate / research / pause verdict

## Premium Version
